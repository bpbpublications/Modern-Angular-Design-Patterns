Chapter 10 — Forms and User Input

Template-driven vs reactive forms, form state tracking, built-in validators, the Builder pattern for dynamic forms, the Strategy pattern for validation logic, and submission workflows — every form below is fully interactive with visible validation states.


  • Enable with provideZonelessChangeDetection() at bootstrap, then remove zone.js from the polyfills and uninstall it.
  • Standard form flows need nothing special: bound listeners, signals and markForCheck() (via AsyncPipe) trigger change detection.
  • Watch out for manual DOM listeners and NgZone observables (onStable etc.) — replace with signals, afterNextRender/afterEveryRender, or MutationObserver.
  • OnPush is not required but aligns well with zoneless patterns.
